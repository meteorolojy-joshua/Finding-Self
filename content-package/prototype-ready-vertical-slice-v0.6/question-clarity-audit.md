# Questions that are difficult to understand — v0.6 audit

Review persona: an average user reading quickly, with limited tolerance for unusual phrasing, and with English as an additional language.

Reviewed 323 user-facing question records: 128 routed screens, 189 inventory questions, and 6 distinct onboarding questions.

Flagged and revised 178: 94 routed screens, 81 inventory questions, and 3 onboarding questions.

Every unlisted question was reviewed and judged understandable without a rewrite. Answers, routes, IDs, and default-option membership were not edited.
